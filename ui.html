1. Upload and settings

Default: at most 360 requests/hour (one start every 10 seconds), one active lookup, and a 10-second timeout. A slow lookup is closed and marked failed before the next roll is started in a fresh tab. A 403/429/503 response stops the job immediately. The client keeps one fixed, identifiable user agent; no IP/proxy rotation. Use automated lookups only with the board's permission. A CAPTCHA or other access challenge may mean automated access is not permitted.
